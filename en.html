Video Games

Valorant

In my spare time, I also enjoy playing Valorant. It’s a first-person shooter (FPS) in which two teams of five players compete against each other. In this game, it’s important to be able to communicate clearly as a team and to make quick decisions, whether individually or as a team, in order to win the match. I find it a very interesting game because there’s always something new to learn, and analysing past matches allows you to assess your strengths and weaknesses whilst coming up with new strategies.
